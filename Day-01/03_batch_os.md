#### 1. Batch Operating Systems
A Batch Operating System is an operating system that collects similar jobs, groups them into batches, and executes them one after another without requiring direct user interaction during execution.

In simple word, users prepare their jobs offline (usually using punch cards or files) and submit them to the operatior. operator groups the similar tasks into the batches based on their requirement and submit the batches to OS without direct user involvment and the batch are arranged in the queue (usually the ready queue) for execution.

**Example:**<br>
*Let's understand with a real-world example.*<br>
*Suppose a university needs to generate salary slips for 500 employees.*<br>
*Instead of generating each salary slip manually, the university submits all 500 salary records to the computer.*<br>
**The Batch OS works like this:**<br>
Step 1: Collect jobs<br>
*Salary records of 500 employees*

Step 2: Group jobs into a Batch<br>
*All salary records are grouped together*

Step 3: Execute jobs<br>
*Processes salary records one after another*

Step 4: Generate Output<br>
*500 salary slips are generated*

### Advantages of Batch OS
*Reduces manual intervention.*<br>
*Suitable for processing large anount of data.*<br>
*Efficient for repetitive tasks.*<br>
*Jobs can be processed automatically.*<br>
*Useful for scheduled operations.*

### Disadvantages
Long Waiting Time: *A job may have to wait until its batch is processed.*<br>
Difficult Debugging: *Identifying errors in a batch can be difficult.*<br>
No Immediate Response: *Users don't get immediate results.*<br>
Job Dependency: *A problem in one job can sometimes affect subsequent processing, depending on the system.*

### Real-world applications
Some examples of tasks suitable for batch processing:<br>
*Bank transaction processing.*<br>
*Electrity bill generation.*<br>
*Employee salary processing.*<br>
*University result generation.*<br>
*Bulk data processing.*<br>
*Monthly report generation.*
